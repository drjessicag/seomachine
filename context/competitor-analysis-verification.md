## What This Changes in the Strategy

1. **Re-tier the SEO threats.** Add **Shana James** as HIGH (search) and **Stephen Hedger** as the #1 SEO competitor. Downgrade Fehr from "the article to outrank" to "one of three."
2. **"Win written search where founder coaches don't publish" holds up, with a caveat.** Herzog and Polonska really aren't in search. But founders don't search as founders, so founder-framed titles won't pull search traffic on their own. **Target the husband-side problem query, then frame the article for founders** (for example, "Why Won't My Wife Have Sex With Me? A Chemist's Answer for High-Performing Men").
3. **Drop or deprioritize the "she's checked out" angle for SEO.** At 40/mo it's a copywriting phrase, not a search target. Better targets: "when to walk away from sexless marriage" (1,600), "sexless marriage effect on husband" (880), "responsive desire" (2,400).
4. **"Founder Mode Is Killing Your Marriage" is a social/LinkedIn piece, not an SEO piece.** "founder mode" searchers want Paul Graham's essay.
5. **Own "relationship chemistry" (880/mo, KD 6).** It's already the trademarked method name and the difficulty is low.
6. **Bliss Science starts from near zero** (16 keywords, 18 visits/mo). The existing dead-bedroom post ranks #10–32. It's the best candidate to refresh first.
7. **Fix the David Chambers profile** before relying on it.
8. **Matchmaking is high-value but crowded** ($15.80 CPC in SF). Only enter it with a clear differentiator.
