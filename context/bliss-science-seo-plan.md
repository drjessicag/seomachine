### Month 1 (October): Foundation
1. **Connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics to OpenSEO.** This gives real data instead of estimates, plus the full list of sites that link to you.
2. **Article 1: Refresh "How to Revive a Dead Bedroom."** It already ranks #10–32. Retarget it to "dead bedroom" (3,600 searches/mo, easy difficulty). Expand it with your chemist angle and the "I was that wife" story, and link it to Relationship Reboot.
